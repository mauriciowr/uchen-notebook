# Uchen Notebook · Caderno de Uchen

A small offline web app for learning to **write the Tibetan uchen script** by hand, with a pen or a finger on a tablet.

**Open the app:** https://mauriciowr.github.io/uchen-notebook/

## What it covers

- **The 30 consonants**: trace each letter over a guide, then from memory, with step-by-step stroke order.
- **Vowels**: the four vowel marks (gigu, zhabkyu, drengbu, naro) on their own and combined with each consonant.
- **Superscripts and subscripts**: stacked letters such as རྐ, སྔ, ཀྱ, གྲ and བླ, with example words.
- **Mantras**, syllable by syllable: Om Mani Padme Hung, the Vajra Guru mantra of Guru Rinpoche, and the Vajrakilaya mantra, including Sanskrit-only marks such as ཾ, ཿ, ཱ and ྄.
- **Review cards**: build a deck from the consonant rows you choose. Add vowel combinations and real Tibetan words spelled only with those letters (140-word list). Missed cards come back into the deck soon after.

## Features

- **Two pronunciations**: Amdo (including Golok), with no tones and voiced 3rd-column letters, and Lhasa (central Tibetan), with high and low tones. Descriptions compare sounds to familiar English and Portuguese words. They are a starting point: your teacher's pronunciation always comes first.
- **Shape check**: after you write, the app shows how much of the letter you covered and how much of your stroke stayed on the shape. It does not check stroke order.
- **English and Portuguese interface**: switch with the *English / Português* button.
- **Works offline**: after the first visit, everything (including the Tibetan font) is stored on the device. Install it from the browser menu (*Install app* / *Add to Home screen*) to open it full screen from an icon.
- **Sync button**: fetches the latest version of the app and, optionally, merges your progress across devices through a **private gist** in your own GitHub account. This needs a fine-grained token with *Gists: read and write* permission only. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Sources

- Stroke order: Chris Fynn, [How to write the Tibetan script](https://sites.google.com/view/chrisfynn/home/tibetanscriptfonts/howtowritethetibetanscript)
- [Tibetan Language Institute calligraphy guide (PDF)](https://www.tibetanlanguage.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Tibetan-Alphabet-Calligraphy-Guide-PDF.pdf)
- Fonts: Noto Serif Tibetan, Literata and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License), bundled through [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org)

Mantra spellings and recitation vary slightly between lineages; check them against your own practice texts.

## Em português

Um app para aprender a **escrever o tibetano uchen** à mão, com caneta ou dedo no tablet. Ele cobre as 30 consoantes com a ordem dos traços, as vogais, as letras sobrescritas e subscritas, os mantras (Om Mani, Guru Rinpoche e Vajrakilaya) e cards de revisão com palavras. Tem pronúncia de Amdo/Golok e de Lhasa e funciona offline. O botão **⟳ Sincronizar** busca a versão nova e leva o progresso entre aparelhos por uma gist privada.
